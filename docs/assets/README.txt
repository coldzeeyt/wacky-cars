Drop your assets here:

  car.png    - your car sprite (PNG with transparency).
               Tune SPRITE_TARGET_W in js/car.js to match its size.
               Set HIDE_WHEELS_WITH_SPRITE = true if the art includes wheels.

  music.mp3  - looping background music. Plays on first Start/Creator tap
               (browsers block autoplay until you interact). Mute toggle is
               on the menu and in the play HUD.

Both are optional - the game runs fine without them (drawn car + no music).
